# Acknowledgment Templates — every one of these is a DRAFT

**Every template below produces a DRAFT that requires your explicit approval before sending.**
Personalize from the lead's own words (mirror their stated need); fill every `{{field}}`; never
invent facts, availability, or pricing. Keep it short — the goal is a fast, human, credible reply.

>
> Happy to show you exactly how we handle that. I have {{slot_option_1}} or {{slot_option_2}}
> open this week — does either work? If it's easier, grab any time here: {{scheduling_link}}.
>
> {{sender_name}}

## Rules

- **DRAFT only** — none of these are sent without explicit human approval (show recipient + copy).
- No fabricated names, calendars, case studies, or numbers; `{{fields}}` come from real data or
  stay visibly unfilled for the human to complete.
- No pricing, discounts, or contractual commitments in an acknowledgment — that's a gated
  follow-up with CFO/CLO input (`.claude/agents/cro-sales.md` charter).
- Match the lead's language and formality; don't over-sell a COLD lead.
