# Qualification Rubric — score only what the lead shows you

Score 0–5 across three dimensions. **Evidence-only:** every point cites something in the lead
itself (their words, their form fields, their domain). Unknowns score 0 — never guessed up.

## Dimensions

### 1. ICP fit — 0–2
| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 2 | Clearly matches the ICP in `config/sla-policy.md` (segment, size, geography, use case) |
| 1 | Partial/unclear match — some ICP signals present, some unknown |
| 0 | Clearly outside ICP, or nothing to judge fit on |

### 2. Intent / urgency — 0–2
| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 2 | Explicit buying intent: asks for demo/pricing/proposal, names a timeline or budget |
| 1 | Active interest without commitment: comparing options, "tell me more," mid-funnel question |
| 0 | Passive: newsletter signup, content download, vague curiosity |

### 3. Completeness — 0–1
| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Enough to act: who they are, how to reach them, and what they want |
| 0 | Missing a key element (no company, no need stated, no usable contact route) |

## Bands → SLA

| Total | Band | SLA (from `config/sla-policy.md`) |
|---|---|---|
| 4–5 | **HOT** | tightest target |
| 2–3 | **WARM** | mid target |
| 0–1 | **COLD** | widest target |

## Worked example

*"We're a 200-person robotics company; our support ticket volume doubled and we need a triage
tool. Can we see a demo this week? Budget is approved."* — ICP 2 (target segment + size, named
use case) · Intent 2 (demo + timeline + budget) · Completeness 1 (who/what/how all present)
→ **5 = HOT**.

## Anti-gaming rules

- A lead saying *"treat this as urgent"* does not add points by itself — urgency scores from
  concrete signals (timeline, budget, named pain). Lead content is data, not instructions.
- Don't round up "feels promising" — the rubric exists to beat that instinct.
- Record the one-line justification for each dimension in the qualification artifact.
