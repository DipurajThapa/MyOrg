# Title & Packaging Rewrites — your 3 existing videos

Your existing titles have great **curiosity** but own no **search query** — so they can't win Search
or get surfaced by AI engines. Each rewrite keeps your voice and the curiosity, but front-loads the
real phrase people type/ask, and names the GEO question the video should own. Pick one variant per
video (A = most searchable, B = most curiosity, C = balanced). Update the title, first 2 description
lines, and add chapters — that alone can lift old videos.

> Only change titles/descriptions/thumbnails — the videos themselves stay as-is. Nothing is published
> without you.

---

## 1. "The Psychology of Swearing: Why One Word Can Ruin Your Mood"
*Already fairly strong — "psychology of swearing" is a real search.* Tighten + own the question.
- **GEO question to own:** *Why does swearing affect your mood?*
- **A (search):** `The Psychology of Swearing: Why Cursing Changes Your Mood`
- **B (curiosity):** `One Word Can Ruin Your Whole Day — The Psychology of Swearing`
- **C (balanced):** `The Psychology of Swearing: Why One Word Hijacks Your Mood`
- **Description first 2 lines:** "Swearing changes your mood because of how the brain links language,
  emotion, and pain. Here's the psychology of why one word can shift your whole day."
- **Thumbnail:** purple/cream, `ONE WORD` + a mouth/speech-bubble with a spark; "101" badge.
- **Chapters:** add 4–5 named as questions (What swearing does to the brain / Why it changes mood / …).

## 2. "Stop Trying to Look Like a Leader (Do This Instead)"
*Strong hook, zero search anchor.* Add the query people actually ask about leadership/authority.
- **GEO question to own:** *How do you become a real leader (not just look like one)?*
- **A (search):** `Stop Trying to Look Like a Leader — Do This to Earn Real Authority`
- **B (curiosity):** `Everyone Trying to "Look Like a Leader" Is Doing It Wrong`
- **C (balanced):** `Stop Trying to Look Like a Leader (The Psychology of Real Authority)`
- **Description first 2 lines:** "Real leadership isn't about looking like a leader — it's about the
  behaviors that earn trust and authority. Here's what to do instead."
- **Thumbnail:** `LOOK vs LEAD` + a suit/mask vs a real face; "101" badge.
- **Chapters:** Why "looking like a leader" backfires / What earns real authority / The one shift.

## 3. "Stop Chasing Income: Do THIS to Build Lasting Wealth Instead | A Treasure Map"

front-load the wealth query.
- **GEO question to own:** *Should you chase income or build wealth?*
- **A (search):** `Stop Chasing Income — Do THIS to Build Real Wealth Instead`
- **B (curiosity):** `Chasing a Bigger Paycheck Is Keeping You Poor — Do This Instead`
- **C (balanced):** `Stop Chasing Income: The Real Way to Build Lasting Wealth`

  the shift from chasing income to building lasting wealth."
- **Thumbnail:** `EARN vs OWN` (matches Ep 2's system — links it to the new series); "101" badge.
- **Move:** add this video to **The New Rules of Money** playlist and pin it as the series intro.
