# Shorts Plan — The New Rules of Money

From ~10 subs, **Shorts are your fastest follower engine.** Each long episode yields 2–3 Shorts.
Goal: reach (Shorts) → route to the long video/playlist (pin + end card) → subscribers.

## The Shorts system
- **Cut 3 moment types** from each episode: (1) the **cold-open hook**, (2) the **most surprising
  claim / one-liner**, (3) the **one actionable flip**. Each already exists in the script.
- **Length:** 20–40s. **Hook in the first 1–2 seconds** (Shorts are ruthless).
- **Format:** the punch line as big on-screen text over simple motion/b-roll; voiceover reads it.
- **Brand:** purple/cream + "101" badge so Shorts and long-form read as one channel.
- **CTA:** end card + pinned comment → "Full breakdown in Episode N — link in comments/playlist."
- **Caption:** lead with the question (`Why do smart people stay broke?`) for search + GEO.
- **Cadence:** post Shorts *between* long uploads to stay in-feed daily without burning episodes.

| Ep | Short A (the surprising claim) | Short B (the flip) |
