### [0:00–0:10] COLD-OPEN HOOK
**VO:** "Forget every money hack you've saved — the apps, the coupon tricks, the side-hustle threads.
Wealth doesn't come from a hack. It comes from one number nobody names out loud: the gap. And right
now, yours is probably invisible."
**[ON-SCREEN:** THE GAP]** · [two bars — EARN and SPEND — almost touching, a thin sliver between]

### [0:10–0:30] GEO ANSWER BLOCK  ← citation target
**VO:** "Here's the short answer to how you actually build wealth. Wealth is the gap between what you
earn and what you spend — and what you do with it. Not your salary. Not your title. The gap. Most
people attack one side: earn more, *or* spend less. The new rule uses both, then adds the step nobody
talks about — you convert that gap into assets that pay you. Earn, keep a gap, turn the gap into
income, repeat. That's the whole formula."
**[ON-SCREEN:** Wealth = (Earn − Spend) → Assets → income → wider gap]**

### [0:30–0:42] VALUE PROMISE + OPEN LOOP
**VO:** "Coming up: why chasing income alone fails, why cutting costs hits a wall, and the loop that
grows a small gap on its own. Stay to the end — the reason most gaps never grow isn't math. It's a
trap that feels exactly like progress."

### [0:42–1:25] BODY 1 — The two half-rules
**VO:** "Let's kill the two half-rules. 'Earn more' sounds like the answer — until the raise lands.
Then the nicer car, the bigger place, the upgraded everything. You earn more, and the gap stays
exactly the same."
**[PATTERN INTERRUPT:** record-scratch → "same gap, bigger bills"]**
**VO:** "So people flip to 'spend less.' But cutting has a floor — you can't spend below zero. Earning
has no ceiling; saving has a floor. One side alone never gets you there. The gap is the thing — not the
paycheck, not the penny-pinching, the *space* between them."
**[ON-SCREEN:** You don't get wealthy on one side of the equation. You get wealthy on the gap.]**
> A bigger income with the same gap is just a more expensive way to stay broke.

### [1:25–2:10] BODY 2 — The Gap → Asset Loop  (original element)
**VO:** "Here's the part almost everyone misses. A gap on its own is just cash sitting still. It feels
safe, but it's asleep. The move that changes everything is the **Gap → Asset Loop.** You take the gap
and buy something that produces income. That income lands on the *earn* side — money you didn't work
an hour for. Which makes the gap wider, and buys the next asset faster."
**[VISUAL:** a coin dropped into a small machine that spits two coins back, feeding the next machine]**
**VO:** "Gap funds asset. Asset feeds gap. The first turn is painfully slow — but a loop doesn't add,
it compounds. Your job isn't to earn your way there or save your way there. It's to keep the loop
turning."
**[ON-SCREEN:** Gap → Asset → income → wider Gap → repeat]**
> Cash sitting still is a gap asleep. An asset is a gap that went to work.

### [2:10–2:28] MID-ROLL RE-HOOK
**VO:** "Now notice something. Everything so far assumes your gap grows when your income does. For most
people, it doesn't — and they never see it happen. There's a trap between your next raise and your
wealth, and it's dangerous precisely because it feels like winning. Let's name it."

### [2:28–3:20] BODY 3 — The trap that feels like progress  (open-loop payoff)
**VO:** "Here it is: **lifestyle creep.** Every time your income rises, your spending quietly rises to
match it. New money in, new expenses out. Your paycheck grows, your lifestyle grows, and the gap never
moves. It feels like progress — but the number that builds wealth stays frozen at zero."
**[ON-SCREEN:** Lifestyle creep = a raise that never reaches the gap]**
**VO:** "So here's your one job. Next time your income goes up — a raise, a bonus, a new client — don't
let your spending follow automatically. Send a fixed slice straight into the gap *before* you feel it.
Let your lifestyle rise slower than your income."
> When your income rises, let your lifestyle rise slower. The difference is where wealth lives.

### [3:20–4:00] CLOSE — PAYOFF + CTA + NEXT EPISODE
**VO:** "So — wealth was never the salary or the budget. It's the gap, and what you do with it. Widen
it on both sides, protect it from creep, and run the loop: gap into asset, asset into income, income
into a wider gap. Your move this week: catch your next bit of extra income before it disappears into
your lifestyle — and route it to the gap. Do that once, and you've beaten the trap most people never
see. Next: the question everyone asks the moment the gap grows — should you save it, or invest it, and
in what order? Episode 11 gives you the order that keeps you from quitting. Subscribe so you don't miss
it. New rules only."
**[ON-SCREEN:** End screen — Ep 11 "Save or Invest First?" + Subscribe]** · Pinned comment:
