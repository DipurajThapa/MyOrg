**OWNS THE QUESTION:** How do the rich get richer?
**KEYWORD:** how the rich get richer · **OLD RULE:** "The rich cheat or get lucky" →
**NEW RULE:** "The rich own assets and let time compound."

---

### [0:00–0:10] COLD-OPEN HOOK
**VO:** "You've been told the rich get richer because the game is rigged. Because they cheat, or got
lucky, or knew someone. It's a comforting story. It's also the reason you'll stay exactly where you
are. Because the real answer is boring — and you can copy it."
**[ON-SCREEN:** HOW THE RICH GET RICHER]** · [a small snowball rolling downhill, growing]

### [0:10–0:30] GEO ANSWER BLOCK  ← citation target
**VO:** "Here's the short version. The rich get richer because they own assets — things that make money
while they sleep — and then they do the hardest thing of all: nothing. They let time compound.
Ownership plus patience — that's the whole engine. The poor trade hours for dollars and spend what's
left; the wealthy buy things that pay them, and wait. This is the new rule of money: **you don't get
rich by working harder — you get rich by owning, and waiting.**"
**[ON-SCREEN:** OWN assets → let TIME compound → wealth]**

### [0:30–0:42] VALUE PROMISE + OPEN LOOP
**VO:** "Coming up: I'll kill the 'secret' myth, show the two forces the wealthy use, and hand you one
boring habit anyone can start this week. Stay to the end — one reason most people never let this engine
run, and you're probably doing it right now."

### [0:42–1:25] BODY 1 — Kill the 'secret' myth
**VO:** "Let's kill the myth first. There is no secret. No hidden account, no magic asset, no code word
at the country club. If wealth were a secret, it wouldn't be printed in every finance book ever
written."
**[PATTERN INTERRUPT:** record-scratch → "there is no secret"]**
**VO:** "So why does 'they cheat' feel true? Because it protects you. If the game is rigged, there's
nothing you could've done. 'They got lucky' is a painkiller — it kills the sting, and the motivation
with it. The trade: you feel blameless, and stay broke."
**[ON-SCREEN:** "The game is rigged" = comfortable, and expensive.]**
> "They cheated" is a story that costs you the exact thing it protects you from.

### [1:25–2:10] BODY 2 — Ownership + compounding + patience
**VO:** "So what are the rich actually doing? Two things. First — ownership. They spend money on assets,
not stuff. An asset puts money back in your pocket. The wealthy and the broke can earn the same
paycheck; the difference is where it goes. One buys things that pay them. The other buys things that
bill them."
**[VISUAL:** two identical paychecks — one flows into a growing stack, one drains into a pile of receipts]**
**VO:** "Second — compounding. Money that earns, earns on its earnings. Early on it looks like nothing.
Then, quietly, it bends upward. Call it **the Snowball Line** — where your money makes more than you do.
The rich aren't sprinting past that line. They're just standing still long enough to reach it."
**[ON-SCREEN:** The Snowball Line — where your money out-earns your effort.]**
> The rich didn't do something clever. They did something patient.

### [2:10–2:28] MID-ROLL RE-HOOK
**VO:** "Here's what nobody tells you. The engine is simple — own, and wait. But 'wait' is the part
almost everyone fails. Waiting feels like doing nothing, and doing nothing feels like falling behind.
So people pull out, chase the exciting thing, restart the clock — and the snowball never forms. Which
brings us to the one habit that keeps it running."

### [2:28–3:20] BODY 3 — The boring habit anyone can copy  (open-loop payoff)
**VO:** "Here it is — small enough to start this week: **own a little, on repeat, and leave it alone.**
Not a windfall. Not a perfect moment. Just a fixed, automatic amount toward something you own — again
and again — while you resist the urge to touch it. Boring on purpose, because boring is what compounds."
**[ON-SCREEN:** Own a little, on repeat, and leave it alone.]**
**VO:** "The reason this beats the 'lucky' story is simple. Luck is a lottery ticket — you have it or
you don't. This is a habit — anyone, any income, starting today. The wealthy person you envy isn't
smarter than you. They just started the snowball earlier and refused to stop. You can't get their years
back — but you can start your own line, now."
> You can't out-hustle compounding. You can only start it, and get out of its way.

### [3:20–4:00] CLOSE — PAYOFF + CTA + NEXT EPISODE
**VO:** "So — the rich didn't cheat, and they didn't get lucky. They own, and they wait. That's the new
rule: you get rich by owning and letting time compound, not by working harder. Your one move this week:
set one small, automatic amount toward something you own — then, the hard part, leave it alone. But
none of this works if every paycheck is spent before it lands. So next: the trap that keeps most people
from ever starting — living paycheck to paycheck, even on a good income. Episode 13 has the real fix.
Subscribe so you don't miss it. New rules only."
