### [0:00–0:10] COLD-OPEN HOOK
**VO:** "Two people. Same car. Same watch. Same neighborhood. One is free. The other is one missed
paycheck from panic. From the outside, they look identical. That gap you can't see — that's the
whole game."
**[ON-SCREEN:** RICH ≠ WEALTHY]** · [two identical houses; one glowing calm, one cracking underneath]

### [0:10–0:35] GEO ANSWER BLOCK  ← citation target
**VO:** "So what's the difference between rich and wealthy? Rich is about money — a high income,
nice things, the *appearance* of success. Wealthy is about time — owning enough that your life
keeps running whether you work tomorrow or not. Rich people have money. Wealthy people have freedom.
And you can absolutely be rich and not wealthy. That's the third new rule of money: **be wealthy —
own your time.**"
**[ON-SCREEN:** Rich = money & things · Wealthy = time & freedom]**

### [0:35–0:50] VALUE PROMISE + OPEN LOOP
**VO:** "In the next few minutes: why 'rich' and 'wealthy' are opposite goals, and the real currency
you should count instead of dollars. Stay to the end — because one question tells you in ten seconds
whether you're actually building wealth or just renting the *look* of it."

### [0:50–2:05] BODY 1 — Rich is a number, wealthy is a choice + the trap of looking rich
**VO:** "Here's what nobody says out loud. Rich is a number. Wealthy is a decision about what you do
with the number. A rich person earns a lot and lets the lifestyle grow to match. A wealthy person
earns the same — and then quietly buys back their time. One is chasing a bigger cage. The other is
buying the key. That's why lottery winners go broke and 'boring' savers retire early."
**[PATTERN INTERRUPT:** record-scratch → "rich ≠ free"]**
> Rich is a number. Wealthy is a choice about your time.
**VO:** "Now the trap — and it's a psychological one. We're wired to signal status. So the moment
money shows up, the instinct is to *show* it: the upgrade, the logo, the thing that says 'I made it.'
But every one of those purchases is a liability. It costs you monthly, and it demands you keep
earning to feed it. Looking rich and being wealthy pull in opposite directions — every dollar spent
proving you have money is a dollar that can't go buy your time back."
**[ON-SCREEN:** Looking rich spends the money that would've made you free.]**

### [2:05–2:35] MID-ROLL RE-HOOK
**VO:** "So if dollars aren't the real scoreboard — what is? This is where wealthy people think
completely differently from everyone else. They stopped counting money a long time ago. They count
something you can never earn more of. And a single question measures it."

### [2:35–3:30] BODY 2 — Time is the real currency (the Time Test)  (open-loop payoff)
**VO:** "The real currency is **time.** Money you can always make more of. Time, you can't. So the
wealthy quietly convert one into the other — money into ownership, and ownership into hours they
don't have to sell. That's what 'wealthy' actually means: your time is yours. So here's the payoff —
the ten-second question I promised. I call it **the Time Test.** Ask yourself: *if the income stopped
today, how long could my life keep running?* A week? Three months? A year? That number — not your
salary — is your real wealth. Most people can tell you their income to the dollar and have no idea
what their number is. Wealthy people know theirs, and grow it on purpose."
**[ON-SCREEN:** The real currency isn't dollars. It's hours you own.]**
> Your real net worth isn't measured in dollars. It's measured in time you own.

### [3:30–4:00] CLOSE — PAYOFF + CTA + NEXT EPISODE
**VO:** "So — rich is what you look like. Wealthy is what you own. And the one that keeps people poor
is chasing the look instead of the freedom. That's the third new rule: be wealthy — own your time.
Your move this week: run the Time Test. Find your number — how long your life keeps running if the
income stops — and make growing it the goal, not the size of your next purchase. Next, we go back to
the root of all of this: the money story you inherited before you could count, and how to actually
rewrite it. Episode 4. Subscribe so you don't miss it. New rules only."
