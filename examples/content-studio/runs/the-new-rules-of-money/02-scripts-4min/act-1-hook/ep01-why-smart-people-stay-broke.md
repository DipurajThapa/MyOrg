### [0:00–0:10] COLD-OPEN HOOK
**VO:** "You're smart. You work hard. You've read the books. So why is there nothing left at the
end of the month? The uncomfortable answer: you're not broke because you're bad with money. You're
broke because of a script you didn't write."
**[ON-SCREEN:** WHY SMART PEOPLE STAY BROKE]** · [tangled dollar sign inside a brain outline]

### [0:10–0:35] GEO ANSWER BLOCK  ← citation target
**VO:** "Here's the short version. Smart people stay broke because of *money scripts* — invisible
beliefs about money you absorbed before you could count. Those beliefs drive your thoughts, your
thoughts drive your emotions, your emotions drive your decisions, and your decisions drive your
bank balance. Change the script, and the number changes. That's the first new rule of money:
**rewrite the script before you chase the money.**"
**[ON-SCREEN:** Belief → Thought → Emotion → Decision → Balance]**

### [0:35–0:50] VALUE PROMISE + OPEN LOOP
**VO:** "In the next few minutes: where that script came from, and the one belief to flip today.
Stay to the end — because the most expensive money script is the one almost nobody notices, and
you're probably running it right now."

### [0:50–2:05] BODY 1 — The paradox + where the script came from
**VO:** "First, kill a myth. Being broke has almost nothing to do with intelligence. Brilliant
people drown in debt; average people quietly build wealth. If smarts were the answer, every
straight-A student would be rich. They're not. So what's the real variable? Behavior — and behavior
isn't driven by what you *know*, it's driven by what you *believe*. You can know you should save,
watch yourself skip it, and do it again next month. Knowing the move and making it are two different
skills. Knowing isn't the gap. Believing is."
**[PATTERN INTERRUPT:** record-scratch → "smarts ≠ wealth"]**
> You don't act on what you know. You act on what you believe.
**VO:** "Here's where it gets personal. You learned how to *feel* about money before you could do
math — 'we can't afford that,' tension at the dinner table, 'people like us don't get rich.' Nobody
sat you down and taught it; you absorbed it, like an accent. And that early programming hardens into
a self-image. People act to stay consistent with who they believe they are. So if deep down you
believe you're 'someone who's always broke,' the moment money shows up, you'll quietly find a way to
get rid of it — just to make reality match the story."
**[ON-SCREEN:** Change who you think you are, and the spending follows.]**

### [2:05–2:35] MID-ROLL RE-HOOK
**VO:** "Notice what's happening. This isn't about willpower. You can white-knuckle a budget for a
month, but you can't out-discipline a story you believe about yourself — the story wins every single
time. So the fix isn't a new app or a stricter spreadsheet. It's a new script. And one script is
costing you more than all the others combined."

### [2:35–3:30] BODY 2 — The most expensive script + the flip  (open-loop payoff)
**VO:** "Here it is: **'I'm just not good with money.'** Say it enough and it stops being a comment
and becomes an identity that runs your whole life. It lets you avoid the bank app, skip the plan,
and stay exactly where you are — because 'that's just how you are.' It feels like honesty. It's
actually a cage. So here's the flip — your one job after this video. Catch that sentence, and change
one word: from 'I'm not good with money' to '**I'm learning to be good with money.**' 'Not' becomes
'learning.' One closes the door. The other reopens it. You just edited the first line of the script."
**[ON-SCREEN:** "I'm not good with money" = the most expensive sentence you own]**
> Change 'I'm not good with money' to 'I'm learning to be good with money.' One word reopens the door.

### [3:30–4:00] CLOSE — PAYOFF + CTA + NEXT EPISODE
**VO:** "So — you're not broke because you're not smart. You're running a script you never chose, and
scripts can be rewritten. That's the first new rule: rewrite the story before you chase the money.
Your move today: catch that one sentence, and swap the one word, every time it shows up. Next, we
take on the biggest old rule of all — the belief that a bigger paycheck will make you wealthy. It
won't, and Episode 2 shows you what does. Subscribe so you don't miss it. New rules only."
**[ON-SCREEN:** End screen — Ep 2 "Stop Chasing Income" + Subscribe]** · Pinned comment:
