# Growth Plan — The New Rules of Money (for @TheNewRules101)

*`youtube-script-writer` · DEDICATED mode. Tuned to your real state: **~10 subscribers, launch
stage.** At this size the game is **volume + consistency + a bingeable series + Shorts + GEO** — not
per-video micro-optimization yet.*

## The single biggest lever right now
**Ship this 15-episode series on a fixed cadence, cut Shorts from every episode, and build the binge
playlist — while fixing your 3 existing videos' packaging.** That turns a 3-video start into a
catalog the algorithm can promote, a session viewers can binge, and a body of answers AI engines can
cite. Everything below supports that.

## The stack (in order of leverage at your stage)
1. **Consistency beats brilliance.** Pick a cadence you can hold (1–2/week) and never miss. A new
   channel earns algorithmic trust through reliability. Batch-produce so you don't slip.
2. **Packaging shelf.** Lock the purple/cream + "101" badge thumbnail template (see `03-thumbnails/`).
   Make 3 variants for Ep 1 and Ep 15; A/B the thumbnails. Aim CTR **4–6%+**.
3. **Retention is your existing strength — keep it.** Your ~5-min essays with strong "Stop X / Why X"
   hooks already fit retention-first. Watch the first-30s drop-off and rewrite weak intros next time.
4. **Binge playlist.** Put all 15 in one ordered playlist; every script end-screens into the next.
   Front-loaded Eps 1–3 (highest curiosity) pull viewers into the deeper catalog → session time lifts
   the whole channel.
5. **Shorts → long-form funnel (your fastest follower engine from 10 subs).** Cut 2–3 Shorts per
   episode (see `05-shorts/`). Shorts get reach; pin a comment + end card routing to the long video/
   playlist. Shorts grow *subscribers*; long-form grows *watch time*.
6. **GEO distribution (most creators skip it — you shouldn't).** Money-psychology questions are
   heavily asked to AI engines. Repurpose each transcript into a companion article/FAQ (first 200
   words = the answer; FAQ from the research questions; embed the video; link the playlist). Because
   AI-cited sources now overlap <20% with top Google links, these can get you cited → new viewers.
7. **Fix the back-catalog.** Apply `06-channel-fixes/title-rewrites.md` to your 3 existing videos and
   add video #3 ("Stop Chasing Income") to the money playlist as the intro. Free lift on videos you
   already have.
8. **Engagement velocity.** Each script ends with one specific pinned question; reply to every comment
   in the first hour.

## 30-day launch plan
- **Wk 1:** Fix the 3 existing titles/thumbnails + create the playlist. Publish Ep 1 (flagship) + 3
  Shorts + draft its companion article. Reply to all hour-1 comments; pin the question.
- **Wk 2:** Eps 2–4 on cadence; Shorts for each; publish Ep 1's companion article. Day-7: re-thumbnail

- **Wk 4:** Eps 10–15; Ep 15 capstone gets flagship packaging + a "want Series 2?" subscribe+comment
  ask. Review which topic/hook had the best retention → that's your Series 2 seed.

## Monetization note
Personal finance is **YouTube's highest-CPM niche (~$15–22)** — so watch time here is worth 3–5× an
entertainment channel's, and it feeds your Courses funnel. Grow watch time first; monetize via the
funnel once you're consistent.

## Compliance (money channel)
Keep everything **educational/mindset** — no specific investment advice, no guaranteed returns, no
"financial advisor" framing. If a script drifts toward "buy this asset," flag it for a rewrite
(route to the `clo-legal` agent for anything borderline).

## Governance
Scripts, thumbnails, and this plan only. **Publishing, uploading, or scheduling to YouTube needs your
explicit approval** — the studio produces; you publish.
