**OWNS THE QUESTION:** Why do smart people stay broke?
**KEYWORD:** why smart people stay broke · **OLD RULE:** "Work hard and the money follows" →

### [0:00–0:12] COLD-OPEN HOOK
**VO:** "You're smart. You work hard. You've read the books. So why is there still nothing left at
the end of the month? Here's the uncomfortable answer: you're not broke because you're bad with
money. You're broke because of a script you didn't write."
**[ON-SCREEN:** WHY SMART PEOPLE STAY BROKE]** · [tangled dollar sign inside a brain outline]

### [0:12–0:35] GEO ANSWER BLOCK  ← citation target
**VO:** "Here's the short version. Smart people stay broke because of *money scripts* — the
invisible beliefs about money you absorbed before you could count. Those beliefs drive your
thoughts, your thoughts drive your emotions, your emotions drive your decisions, and your
decisions drive your bank balance. Change the script, and the number changes. This is the first
new rule of money: **rewrite the script before you chase the money.**"
**[ON-SCREEN:** Belief → Thought → Emotion → Decision → Balance]**

### [0:35–0:55] VALUE PROMISE + OPEN LOOP
**VO:** "In the next few minutes I'll show you where that script came from, the exact loop that
keeps smart people stuck, and the one belief to flip today. Stay to the end — because the most
expensive money script is the one almost nobody notices, and you're probably running it right now."

### [0:55–2:05] BODY 1 — The paradox
**VO:** "Let's kill a myth first. Being broke has almost nothing to do with intelligence. There
are brilliant people drowning in debt and average people quietly building wealth. If smarts were
the answer, every straight-A student would be rich. They're not."
**[PATTERN INTERRUPT:** record-scratch → "smarts ≠ wealth"]**
**VO:** "So what's the real variable? Behavior. And behavior isn't driven by what you *know* — it's
driven by what you *believe*. You can know you should save and still not do it. Knowing isn't the
gap. Believing is."
**[ON-SCREEN:** You don't act on what you know. You act on what you believe.]**
> You don't act on what you know. You act on what you believe.

### [2:05–3:15] BODY 2 — Where the script came from
**VO:** "Here's where it gets personal. You learned how to *feel* about money before you could do
math. Maybe you heard 'we can't afford that' at every store. Maybe money meant fighting. Maybe it
meant 'people like us don't get rich.' Nobody sat you down and taught it — you absorbed it, like an
accent."
**[VISUAL:** child at a checkout, tension on the parents' faces]**
**VO:** "And that early programming becomes a self-image. Psychologists who study this find
something wild: people act to stay consistent with who they believe they are. So if deep down you
believe you're 'someone who's always broke,' then the moment money shows up, you'll find a way to
get rid of it — an emergency, a splurge, a bad deal — just to make reality match the story."
> When wealth starts to arrive, a broke self-image quietly sends it back.

### [3:15–3:55] MID-ROLL RE-HOOK
**VO:** "Notice what's happening here. This isn't about willpower. You can white-knuckle a budget
for a month, but you can't out-discipline a story you believe about yourself — the story wins every
time. Which is exactly why the fix isn't a new app. It's a new script. And there's one script
that's costing you the most."

### [3:55–5:00] BODY 3 — The most expensive script + the flip  (open-loop payoff)
**VO:** "Here it is: **'I'm just not good with money.'** Say that enough and it becomes an excuse
that runs your whole life. It lets you avoid the bank app, skip the plan, and stay exactly where
you are — because 'that's just how you are.' It feels like honesty. It's actually a cage."
**[ON-SCREEN:** "I'm not good with money" = the most expensive sentence you own]**
**VO:** "So here's the flip — your one job after this video. Catch that sentence, and change one
word: from 'I'm not good with money' to '**I'm learning to be good with money.**' That's it. One
word — 'not' becomes 'learning.' It sounds small. It isn't. One closes the door. The other reopens
it. You just edited the first line of the script."
> Change 'I'm not good with money' to 'I'm learning to be good with money.' One word reopens the door.

### [5:00–5:40] CLOSE — PAYOFF + CTA + NEXT EPISODE
**VO:** "So — you're not broke because you're not smart. You're running a script you never chose. And
scripts can be rewritten. That's the first new rule: rewrite the story before you chase the money.
Your move today: catch that one sentence, and swap the one word. Do it every time it shows up.
Next, we take on the biggest old rule of all — the belief that a bigger paycheck will make you
wealthy. Spoiler: it won't, and Episode 2 shows you what does. Subscribe so you don't miss it. New
rules only."
**[ON-SCREEN:** End screen — Ep 2 "Stop Chasing Income" + Subscribe]** · Pinned comment:
"What money script did you inherit? Drop it below."
