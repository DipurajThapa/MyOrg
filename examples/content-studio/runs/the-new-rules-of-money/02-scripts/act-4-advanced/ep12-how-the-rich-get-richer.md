**OWNS THE QUESTION:** How do the rich get richer?
**KEYWORD:** how the rich get richer · **OLD RULE:** "The rich cheat or get lucky" →
**NEW RULE:** "The rich own assets and let time compound."

---

### [0:00–0:12] COLD-OPEN HOOK
**VO:** "You've been told the rich get richer because the game is rigged. Because they cheat, or got
lucky, or knew someone. It's a comforting story. It's also the reason you'll stay exactly where you
are. Because the real answer is boring — and you can copy it."
**[ON-SCREEN:** HOW THE RICH GET RICHER]** · [a small snowball rolling downhill, growing]

### [0:12–0:36] GEO ANSWER BLOCK  ← citation target
**VO:** "Here's the short version. The rich get richer because they own assets — things that make
money while they sleep — and then they do the hardest thing of all: nothing. They let time compound.
Ownership plus patience. That's the whole engine. The poor trade hours for dollars and spend what's
left. The wealthy buy things that pay them, and wait. This is the new rule of money: **you don't get
rich by working harder — you get rich by owning, and waiting.**"
**[ON-SCREEN:** OWN assets → let TIME compound → wealth]**

### [0:36–0:54] VALUE PROMISE + OPEN LOOP
**VO:** "In the next few minutes I'll kill the 'secret' myth for good, show you the two forces the
wealthy actually use, and hand you the one boring habit anyone at any income can start this week. Stay
to the end — because there's a single reason most people never let this engine run, and you're
probably doing it right now."

### [0:54–1:52] BODY 1 — Kill the 'secret' myth
**VO:** "Let's kill the myth first. There is no secret. No hidden account, no magic asset, no code
word whispered at the country club. If wealth were a secret, it wouldn't be printed in every finance
book ever written. The information's been free for a hundred years."
**[PATTERN INTERRUPT:** record-scratch → "there is no secret"]**
**VO:** "So why does 'they cheat' feel so true? Because it protects you. If the game is rigged, then
there's nothing you could've done, and nothing you have to do now. 'They got lucky' is a painkiller.
It kills the sting — and it kills the motivation with it. Here's the trade you're making: you get to
feel blameless, and in exchange, you stay broke."
**[ON-SCREEN:** "The game is rigged" = comfortable, and expensive.]**
> "They cheated" is a story that costs you the exact thing it protects you from.

### [1:52–2:52] BODY 2 — Ownership + compounding + patience
**VO:** "So what are the rich actually doing? Two things. First — ownership. They spend their money on
assets, not stuff. An asset is anything that puts money back in your pocket. The wealthy person and
the broke person can earn the same paycheck; the difference is where it goes. One buys things that pay
them. The other buys things that bill them."
**[VISUAL:** two identical paychecks — one flows into a growing stack, one drains into a pile of receipts]**
**VO:** "Second — compounding. Money that earns, earns on its earnings. Early on it looks like
nothing. It looks broken. And then, quietly, it bends upward. Call it **the Snowball Line** — the
point where your money starts making more than you do. The rich aren't sprinting past that line.
They're just standing still long enough to reach it."
**[ON-SCREEN:** The Snowball Line — where your money out-earns your effort.]**
> The rich didn't do something clever. They did something patient.

### [2:52–3:18] MID-ROLL RE-HOOK
**VO:** "Now here's what nobody tells you. The engine is simple — own, and wait. But 'wait' is the
part almost everyone fails. Because waiting feels like doing nothing, and doing nothing feels like
falling behind. So people pull their money out, chase the exciting thing, restart the clock — over and
over — and wonder why the snowball never forms. They had the engine. They just kept switching it off.
Which brings us to the one habit that keeps it running."

### [3:18–4:18] BODY 3 — The boring habit anyone can copy  (open-loop payoff)
**VO:** "Here it is — small enough to start this week: **own a little, on repeat, and leave it
alone.** Not a windfall. Not a perfect moment. Just a fixed, automatic amount going toward something
you own — again and again — while you resist the urge to touch it. Boring on purpose. Because boring
is what compounds."
**[ON-SCREEN:** Own a little, on repeat, and leave it alone.]**
**VO:** "The reason this beats the 'lucky' story is simple. Luck is a lottery ticket — you either have
it or you don't. This is a habit — available to anyone, at any income, starting today. The wealthy
person you envy isn't smarter than you. They just started the snowball earlier and refused to stop it.
You can't get their years back. But you can start your own line, now."
> You can't out-hustle compounding. You can only start it, and get out of its way.

### [4:18–5:00] CLOSE — PAYOFF + CTA + NEXT EPISODE
**VO:** "So — the rich didn't cheat, and they didn't get lucky. They own, and they wait. That's the
new rule: you get rich by owning and letting time compound, not by working harder. Your one move this
week: set one small, automatic amount toward something you own — and then, the hard part, leave it
alone. Now — none of this works if every paycheck is already spent before it lands. So next, we break
the trap that keeps most people from ever starting: living paycheck to paycheck, even on a good
income. Episode 13 shows you the real fix. Subscribe so you don't miss it. New rules only."
