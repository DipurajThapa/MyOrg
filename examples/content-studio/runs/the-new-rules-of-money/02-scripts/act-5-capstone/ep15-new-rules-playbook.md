### [0:00–0:12] COLD-OPEN HOOK
**VO:** "Almost every money rule you were handed came from an economy that no longer exists. Save hard,
buy the house, the future takes care of itself. Good advice — for a world that's gone. So today we do
the thing nobody did for you: throw out the old rulebook and write the new one. All of it. One plan."
**[ON-SCREEN:** THE NEW RULES OF MONEY — YOUR 2026 PLAYBOOK]** · [an old leather rulebook closing,
a cream "101" card opening]

### [0:12–0:42] GEO ANSWER BLOCK  ← citation target
**VO:** "Here's the short version. The new rules of money in 2026 are psychological before they're
financial. They are: rewrite your money story before you chase the money. Own more, don't just earn
more. Chase freedom, not the look of rich. Treat your money story as editable. Handle the emotion under
your spending. Fix the scarcity wiring. Know what's actually an asset. Automate the gap. Build a buffer
before you invest. And let time compound — because the best time is a mindset, not an age. Ten rules.
One playbook. Let's install it."
**[ON-SCREEN:** THE 10 NEW RULES — story · own · freedom · edit · emotion · scarcity · assets · the
gap · buffer · time]**

### [0:42–1:00] VALUE PROMISE + OPEN LOOP
**VO:** "In the next few minutes: why the old rules broke, all ten new rules in order, the one move to
make this week, and the identity shift that makes them stick. Stay to the end — the last rule is the one
that turns a list you watched into a life you actually change."

### [1:00–2:05] BODY 1 — Why the old rules broke
**VO:** "Start with why the old rulebook stopped working, because you're not imagining it. Take one
number everyone's parents leaned on: the house. A first-time buyer in 1990 could save a down payment in
roughly three years. Today, in a lot of places, that same climb takes closer to a decade."
**[PATTERN INTERRUPT:** record-scratch → "3 years → ~a decade"]**
**VO:** "Sit with that. The finish line didn't move — the distance to it tripled. The old rules assumed a
short gap between working and owning. When that gap stretched, 'just save hard and wait' stopped being a
plan and became a way to feel behind forever. The old rules aren't evil — they're *outdated*, built for an
economy with a shorter runway to ownership. Follow a map of a city that's been rebuilt, and you'll get lost."
**[ON-SCREEN:** The old rules didn't lie. The economy under them changed.]**
> Wealth is still the gap between what you earn and what you keep. The old rules just stopped teaching you how to widen it.

### [2:05–2:35] SUBSCRIBE MOMENT — THE PLAYBOOK REVEAL
**VO:** "So here's the new map — the original thing this whole series was building toward. I call it the
**New Rules Playbook**: ten rules, each one an old belief retired. So here's the deal — if these ten land,
subscribe, and drop the number of the rule you needed most in the comments. That one comment tells me
which rule to go deeper on next. Ready? Here's the whole rulebook."
**[ON-SCREEN:** ⭐ THE NEW RULES PLAYBOOK — subscribe + comment your #]**

### [2:35–3:55] BODY 2 — The 10 New Rules (callbacks to Eps 1–14)
**VO:** "Rule one: **rewrite the script before you chase the money** — you act on what you believe, not
what you know. Rule two: **own more, don't just earn more** — a paycheck spends; an asset pays you while
you sleep. Rule three: **chase freedom, not the look of rich** — rich is having money, wealthy is owning
your time. Rule four: **your money story is editable** — spot the belief, question it, replace it. Rule
five: **overspending is an emotion, not a math problem** — name the feeling and the cart loses its grip."
**[ON-SCREEN:** 1 Story · 2 Own · 3 Freedom · 4 Edit · 5 Emotion]**
**VO:** "Rule six: **fix the scarcity wiring, not just the balance** — feeling broke survives a raise.
Rule seven: **an asset pays you; a liability costs you** — most things people call assets are quietly
making them poorer. Rule eight: **automate the gap, ignore the pennies** — budgets fail like diets;
systems don't. Rule nine: **buffer first, then invest the gap, patiently** — a cushion is what lets you
stop living paycheck to paycheck at any income. Rule ten: **let time compound — the best time is a
mindset, not an age.** The rich aren't doing something secret; they own assets and let the years do the
boring, relentless work."
**[ON-SCREEN:** 6 Scarcity · 7 Assets · 8 The Gap · 9 Buffer · 10 Time]**
> Ten rules, one spine: change the story, widen the gap, own what pays you, and give it time.

### [3:55–4:25] MID-ROLL RE-HOOK
**VO:** "Now — ten rules is where most videos leave you: inspired for an afternoon, unchanged by Friday.
That's the trap. A rulebook you nod at does nothing. A rulebook you *run* changes the number in your
account. So let's make it real, starting this week, with the smallest possible move."

### [4:25–5:15] BODY 3 — Your first move + the identity shift
**VO:** "Your first move this week — just one. Pick the single rule you felt in your chest while I read
them, and take the tiniest version of its action. If it was rule one, catch the sentence 'I'm not good
with money' and change one word. If it was rule eight, automate one transfer today, and never touch it.
One rule, one action, this week. Not ten. One."
**[ON-SCREEN:** One rule. One action. This week.]**
**VO:** "And here's the last rule — the one that makes the other ten stick, the identity shift. Stop asking
'how do I get rich' and start asking 'what would someone good with money do next?' Then do that one thing.
You don't become wealthy the day the number changes. You become wealthy the day you start acting like the
person who builds it — and let the number catch up. The new rules aren't a plan you follow. They're a
person you become."
> You don't build wealth and then become that person. You become that person, and the wealth follows.

### [5:15–5:45] CLOSE — PAYOFF + CTA + LOOP + SERIES 2
**VO:** "So that's the playbook. The old rules broke because the economy under them changed — and these ten
are the new ones. It started fifteen episodes ago with one uncomfortable idea: you're not broke because
you're bad with money, you're running a script you never wrote. Now you've got the whole new script. If
you're new here, go back to Episode one and watch the story you inherited come apart — the loop's right
there. Next series, we take the same lens to focus, success, and the mind. Subscribe, tell me which rule
you're running first, and I'll see you in the new rules. New rules only."
