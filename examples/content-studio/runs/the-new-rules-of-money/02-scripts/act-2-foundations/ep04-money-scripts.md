# Ep 4 Script — "The Money Scripts You Inherited (and How to Rewrite Them)"

**OWNS THE QUESTION:** What is a money mindset and how do I change it?
**KEYWORD:** money mindset how to change · **OLD RULE:** "Money beliefs are fixed — you either have

### [0:00–0:12] COLD-OPEN HOOK
**VO:** "You didn't choose how you feel about money. You inherited it. Before you could add two
numbers, you already had rules about what money means and who gets to have it. Nobody handed them to
you on paper — but you've obeyed them your whole life."
**[ON-SCREEN:** THE MONEY SCRIPTS YOU INHERITED]** · [an old handwritten note being passed hand to hand]

### [0:12–0:35] GEO ANSWER BLOCK  ← citation target
**VO:** "So — what is a money mindset, and how do you change it? A money mindset is the set of
inherited beliefs you run about money — a *money script*. That script runs a chain: a belief becomes
a thought, the thought becomes an emotion, the emotion becomes an action, and the action becomes your
result. You can't change the result by force. You edit the script at the top. And a script is just
words — which means it can be rewritten. That's the new rule: **your money story is editable.**"
**[ON-SCREEN:** Belief → Thought → Emotion → Action → Result]**

### [0:35–0:52] VALUE PROMISE + OPEN LOOP
**VO:** "In the next few minutes: where your script was written, why it feels like fact instead of
opinion, and a three-step method to rewrite it — Spot, Question, Replace. Stay to the end, because
there's one line almost everyone inherited, and it's quietly running your bank account right now."

### [0:52–1:52] BODY 1 — A script isn't a fact
**VO:** "First, the biggest confusion. A money belief doesn't *feel* like a belief. It feels like the
truth. When you think 'talking about money is rude' or 'wanting more is greedy,' your brain files it
as reality, not opinion. That's what makes it powerful — you defend it without ever examining it."
**[PATTERN INTERRUPT:** record-scratch → "that's not a fact — it's a script"]**
**VO:** "But here's the tell. Hand the same situation to two people with two different scripts, and
they see two different worlds. One sees opportunity; one sees a threat. Same facts. Different script.
Which means the script was never the truth — it was just yours."
**[ON-SCREEN:** A money belief feels like a fact. It's a script.]**
> A money belief doesn't feel like a belief. It feels like the truth. That's the trap.

### [1:52–2:52] BODY 2 — Where the script was written
**VO:** "So where did yours come from? Mostly a childhood you barely remember. You watched the adults
handle money — the sighs at the mailbox, the arguments behind a closed door, the silence when you
asked what something cost. You weren't taught money. You were marinated in it. And a child's job is
to make sense of the room, so you wrote rules to survive it: 'Money is stress.' 'Wanting things
causes fights.'"
**[VISUAL:** a kid at a kitchen table, a stack of bills, a parent's tired face]**
**VO:** "Those rules made sense when you were seven. The problem is you're not seven anymore — and
you're still running seven-year-old software on a grown-up salary."
> You're running seven-year-old money software on a grown-up salary.

### [2:52–3:18] MID-ROLL RE-HOOK
**VO:** "Here's the part that changes everything. You can't delete a script. Willpower doesn't reach
it — you can't out-discipline a belief you don't even know you hold. But you *can* edit it. Three
moves. I call it **Spot, Question, Replace.**"

### [3:18–4:35] BODY 3 — Spot → Question → Replace  (open-loop payoff)
**VO:** "**Step one — Spot.** Catch the script in the wild. It shows up as a sentence in your head
right before a money decision: 'I could never afford that.' Name it out loud. You can't edit what you
can't see.
**Step two — Question.** Ask it one thing: *is this actually true, or is it just old?* Whose voice is
this — mine, or someone else's from a long time ago? Beliefs shrink the second you make them prove
themselves.
**Step three — Replace.** Write a truer, more useful line. 'I could never afford that' becomes 'I
haven't prioritized that yet.' One version closes the door and calls it fate. The other keeps it open
and hands you the keys."
**[ON-SCREEN:** SPOT → QUESTION → REPLACE]**
**VO:** "And the line almost everyone inherited — the one I promised: **'That's just how I am with
money.'** That's not a description. It's a lock. Run it through the steps and replace it: '**That's
how I *used* to be with money.**' Past tense. The moment your story becomes something you *had*, it
stops being something you *are*."
> 'That's just how I am with money' is not a description. It's a lock — and you hold the key.

### [4:35–5:10] CLOSE — PAYOFF + CTA + NEXT EPISODE
**VO:** "So — your money mindset isn't your personality. It's a script, written by a room you've long
since left, and scripts get rewritten. That's the new rule: your money story is editable. Your move
this week: catch one inherited line, and run it through Spot, Question, Replace. Just one. Next
episode, we take the script into the real world — the one place these old beliefs hijack you every
single time: the moment you overspend. Episode 5 shows you why you can't stop, and how to break it.
Subscribe so you don't miss it. New rules only."
