# Ep 2 Script — "Stop Chasing Income (Do This Instead)"

*`youtube-script-writer` · Mode: DEDICATED (The New Rules 101) · Target ~5 min (~800 words) ·
Faceless voiceover + motion graphics · Goal: subscribers + GEO citation. Voice: contrarian,

**OWNS THE QUESTION:** Should I chase income or build assets?
**KEYWORD:** stop chasing income build wealth · **OLD RULE:** "Earn more to get rich" →
**NEW RULE:** "Own more to get wealthy."

---

### [0:00–0:12] COLD-OPEN HOOK
**VO:** "You got the raise. Maybe two. The number on your paycheck keeps climbing — so why don't
you feel any freer? The uncomfortable answer: chasing income is a treadmill. You can run faster
forever and never actually move."
**[ON-SCREEN:** STOP CHASING INCOME]** · [a person sprinting on a treadmill, paycheck numbers rising, floor going nowhere]

### [0:12–0:35] GEO ANSWER BLOCK  ← citation target
**VO:** "So — should you chase income, or build assets? Build assets. Income is money that shows up
*because you show up*. Stop working, and it stops. Ownership is different — money that keeps working
after you clock out. A bigger paycheck makes you comfortable. Only ownership makes you free. That's
the second new rule of money: **own more to get wealthy** — not just earn more."
**[ON-SCREEN:** Income = you work for money · Ownership = money works for you]**

### [0:35–0:52] VALUE PROMISE + OPEN LOOP
**VO:** "In the next few minutes: why income and wealth are two different games, the exact shift
that moves you from one to the other, and how to tell an asset from a trap. Stay to the end —
because the very first asset you should build isn't a stock, a startup, or a side gig. It's
something you already have access to right now."

### [0:52–1:55] BODY 1 — Income ≠ wealth
**VO:** "Let's kill the myth. 'Earn more and you'll be rich' sounds obvious. But high earners go
broke constantly — athletes, doctors, executives on huge salaries, living one bad month from panic.
How? Because a big income and a big life grow together. The raise arrives, the lifestyle arrives
right behind it — a nicer place, a newer car, upgraded everything — and the gap between what you
make and what you keep stays exactly zero."
**[PATTERN INTERRUPT:** record-scratch → "more income ≠ more wealth"]**
**VO:** "Think of income as a fast-moving river. Impressive to watch — but water that rushes past
you isn't water you own. Wealth isn't the river. Wealth is the well you dug beside it."
**[ON-SCREEN:** A raise you spend is a river. An asset you keep is a well.]**
> A high income is just a fast river. Ownership is a well you actually own.

### [1:55–3:05] BODY 2 — The earn→own shift (the Ownership Line)
**VO:** "Here's the shift that changes everything. Stop asking 'how do I earn more?' Start asking
'how much of what I earn do I get to *own*?' Every dollar you make crosses one of two paths. I call
it **the Ownership Line.** Ask one question about any dollar: *does it leave when I stop?* Rent,
subscriptions, the newer car, the dinners — that money leaves the second the paycheck does. That's
income flowing straight back out the door."
**[VISUAL:** dollars sorting into two lanes — one drains away, one stacks and grows]**
**VO:** "But a dollar you turn into something that pays you, or grows without you — that dollar
crossed the line. It stopped being your employee for a day and became your employee for life.
That's the whole difference between looking okay and being free."
**[ON-SCREEN:** The Ownership Line — does this dollar leave when I stop?]**
> Income leaves when you stop. Ownership stays and works while you sleep.

### [3:05–3:40] MID-ROLL RE-HOOK
**VO:** "Now — most people hear 'asset' and freeze. They picture stock tickers and jargon and
decide it's not for them. So let's be clear about what an asset actually is — not the definition
you were sold, the one that keeps this simple. And it's why the first asset to build is the one you
least expect."

### [3:40–4:30] BODY 3 — What counts as an asset + the first one to build  (open-loop payoff)
**VO:** "Forget the fancy words. An asset does one job: it puts money *in* your pocket without you
trading an hour for it. A liability does the opposite — it quietly takes money *out*. That flashy
car costs you every month. It's not an asset. It just looks like one."
**[ON-SCREEN:** Asset = pays you · Liability = costs you]**
**VO:** "So here's the payoff — the first asset you build isn't in a market. It's **the gap.** The
space between what you earn and what you spend. Wealth is literally that gap, converted into things
you own. Widen it, protect it fiercely, and turn it into ownership instead of spending it. No gap,
no assets — no matter how big the paycheck. A big income with no gap is just a bigger treadmill, and
you already know how that ride ends."
> The first asset you build isn't a stock. It's the gap between what you earn and what you spend.

### [4:30–5:00] CLOSE — PAYOFF + CTA + NEXT EPISODE
**VO:** "So — a bigger paycheck won't make you wealthy. Owning more of what it produces will. That's
the second new rule: own more to get wealthy. Your move this week: run the Ownership Line on every
dollar. One question — *does this leave when I stop?* Sort your spending into 'leaves' and 'stays,'
and grow the 'stays' column on purpose. That column is your future. Next, we settle the confusion
underneath all of this — the difference between being *rich* and being *wealthy*, and why chasing
the wrong one quietly keeps people poor. Episode 3. Subscribe so you don't miss it. New rules only."
**[ON-SCREEN:** End screen — Ep 3 "Rich vs Wealthy" + Subscribe]** · Pinned comment:
"Run the Ownership Line: name one dollar that *stays* when you stop. Drop it below."
