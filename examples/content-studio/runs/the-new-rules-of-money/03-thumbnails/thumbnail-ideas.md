# Thumbnail Ideas — "The New Rules of Money" (15 episodes)

Built on your existing brand so the series looks like *you* on the Browse shelf. Make 2–3 variants
for Ep 1 and Ep 15 and A/B them.

## The shelf system (all 15)
- **Palette:** your channel **purple (~#5B2FD1) + cream/off-white**, plus one bright accent
  (electric yellow) for the key word. High contrast, readable at phone size.
- **The "101" badge:** small cream "101" mark in the **same corner** every time — it ties the series
  to the channel and signals "this is a series → binge."
- **Text:** ≤3 words, huge bold sans (match the channel's heavy numerals). One idea only.
- **One focal visual:** a single symbolic object or a simple psychology metaphor — never a collage.
- **Contrast device:** many of these are "Old vs New" — use a crossed-out old thing + a glowing new thing.
- **Consistency test:** lined up, the 15 read as one franchise.

## Production notes
- 1280×720; test legibility at ~210px. Keep a master template (purple bg + cream "101" badge + text
  style) and swap the focal visual — fast + consistent.
- Concepts, not final art — generate/design, then A/B the two flagships. Re-thumbnail any episode with
  strong retention but weak CTR.
