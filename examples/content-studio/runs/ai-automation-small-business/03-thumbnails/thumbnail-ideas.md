# Thumbnail Ideas — "AI Automation for Small Business" (15 episodes)

Thumbnails decide CTR, and CTR decides whether the algorithm even tries the video. These are
built as a **series "shelf"** — consistent enough to be recognizable in Browse, distinct enough
that each reads instantly. Make 2–3 variants for Ep 1 and Ep 15 and use YouTube's Test & Compare.

## The shelf system (apply to all 15)
- **Palette:** deep navy/charcoal background · one high-visibility accent (electric yellow or
  orange) · white text. High contrast = readable at phone size.
- **Text:** ≤3 words, one idea, huge bold sans-serif. The title says the rest — don't repeat it.
- **One focal element:** a face+emotion OR one clear object/metaphor. Never a busy collage.
- **Series badge:** small consistent `EP 04` tag in the same corner every time (builds the shelf
  + signals "this is a series → binge").
- **Rule of thirds:** text on one side, visual on the other; leave breathing room.
- **Consistency test:** lined up together, the 15 should look like one franchise.

## Per-episode briefs

- **Text:** `SAVE 20 HRS`

- **Emotion:** relief / transformation. **Accent:** yellow on the "20".
- **A/B variants:** (a) just the exhausted→calm face; (b) a clock dissolving into checkmarks;
  (c) "20 HRS" giant with a small figure walking away from a desk.

## Shorts thumbnails (for the cut-downs)
Shorts mostly autoplay, but the cover frame still matters in feeds and the channel grid. Reuse
the episode accent color + a single 1–2 word punch (`24/7`, `$0 → PAID`, `1 HR`). Keep the series
badge so Shorts and long-form read as one channel.

## Production notes
- Design at 1280×720, but check legibility at ~210px wide (mobile Browse) — if the text isn't
  readable there, simplify.
- Keep a master template file (background + badge + text style) and swap the focal visual per
  episode — this is how the shelf stays consistent and fast to produce.
- These are concepts/briefs, not final art. Generate or design them, then **A/B test** the two
  flagships; re-thumbnail any episode with strong retention but weak CTR (see `04-growth/`).
