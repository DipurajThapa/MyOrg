## Fetch by naming convention
Scripts follow `02-scripts/<act>/ep<NN>-<slug>.md` with zero-padded numbers, so they sort and
glob cleanly: `find 02-scripts -name 'ep*.md' | sort`, or grab one act with
`ls 02-scripts/act-3-application/`.
