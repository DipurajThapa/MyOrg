# Growth Plan — The New Rules of Focus (Series 2)

*DEDICATED mode. Same launch-stage playbook as Series 1, plus the Series-2 advantage: you now have
a catalog and a proven format to cross-promote into.*

## The Series-2 edge
Series 2 doesn't start from zero — it starts from Series 1's audience and shelf. Two levers most
new seasons miss:
1. **Cross-promote both ways.** End-screen the last money episodes into Focus Ep 1, and Focus
   episodes back into the money playlist. A viewer who liked the psychology of money will like the
   psychology of focus — same brain, same voice.
2. **One channel identity.** Identical purple/cream "101" thumbnails across both seasons so Browse
   reads as one franchise → more suggested-video pickups.

## The stack (same order of leverage)
1. **Consistency** — hold the cadence; batch-produce.
2. **Packaging shelf** — purple/cream + "101" badge; 3 variants + A/B on Ep 1 and Ep 15. CTR 4–6%+.
3. **Retention** — 5-min essays, strong "Why X / Stop X" hooks (your strength). Watch first-30s drop-off.
4. **Binge playlist** — all 15 in order; end-screen chaining; front-loaded Eps 1–3.
5. **Shorts → long funnel** — 2–3 Shorts/episode (see `05-shorts/`); fastest follower engine.
6. **GEO distribution** — companion article per episode (first 200 words = the answer). Focus/
   procrastination questions are *heavily* asked to AI engines — arguably even more than money.
7. **Cross-series playlists** — a "New Rules of the Mind" mega-playlist combining both seasons for
   marathon session time.
8. **Engagement velocity** — one pinned question per video; reply in hour 1.

## 30-day launch
- **Wk 1:** Focus Ep 1 (flagship) + 3 Shorts + companion article. Add end-screens from money finale → Focus Ep 1.
- **Wk 2:** Eps 2–4; re-thumbnail low-CTR/high-retention videos.
- **Wk 3:** Eps 5–9; push the counterintuitive hooks on Shorts ("Motivation Is a Lie", "Not Lazy").
- **Wk 4:** Eps 10–15; Ep 15 capstone → tease Series 3; review best-retention hook = Series 3 seed.

## Metrics + targets
CTR 4–6%+ · Avg %-viewed ≥40% (~5 min) · Browse+Suggested share rising · Subs/video up ·
Returning-viewer share up (cross-series binge is the tell).

## Compliance
Wellbeing/mindset — not medical advice. If an episode touches clinical territory (ADHD, anxiety
disorders), add "this isn't a diagnosis; talk to a professional." Route anything borderline to the
`clo-legal` agent.

## Governance
Scripts, thumbnails, and this plan only. Publishing needs your explicit approval.
