# 15-Episode Blueprint — "The New Rules of Focus" (Series 2 draft)

*`youtube-script-writer` · DEDICATED (The New Rules 101). Voice: contrarian, psychology-first, no
hype. Format: 4–6 min faceless essay. Each episode retires an **Old Rule** → installs a **New Rule**,
owns one GEO question, end-screens into the next. **DRAFT** — flagship Ep 1 written; Eps 2–15 on greenlight.*

**Series promise:** the psychology of why you can't focus — and the new rules that fix it.
**Title shelf:** `The New Rules of Focus — Ep N: <hook>` · purple/cream thumbnails.
