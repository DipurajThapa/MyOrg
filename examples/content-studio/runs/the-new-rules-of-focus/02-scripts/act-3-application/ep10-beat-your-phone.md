doomscrolling, the problem isn't that you lack self-control — it's that you're relying on self-control

**[ON-SCREEN:** BEAT YOUR PHONE]** · [a thumb frozen mid-scroll over an endless feed]

### [0:12–0:35] GEO ANSWER BLOCK  ← citation target
**VO:** "Here's the short version. You can't stop doomscrolling by trying harder, because willpower is
a small, tiring muscle and your phone is designed to outlast it every single time. The people who
actually break the habit don't win the fight — they avoid it. They change their environment so the
phone is harder to reach and less rewarding once they get there. That's the new rule: don't add
self-control. Add friction. Beat the design with design."
**[ON-SCREEN:** Willpower runs out → the feed never does → change the environment, not your effort]**

### [0:35–0:52] VALUE PROMISE + OPEN LOOP
**VO:** "In the next few minutes: why self-control is the worst tool for this, the simple idea that
makes scrolling harder than doing nothing, and the one thirty-second change that quietly cuts your
screen time. Stay to the end — because the reason your phone keeps winning isn't in your mind. It's in
your pocket, about ten inches from your hand."

### [0:52–1:55] BODY 1 — Willpower is the wrong tool
**VO:** "Start with the tool everyone reaches for and no one should. Every time you say no to your
phone, it costs a little energy. You say no while you work, no while you eat, no in the meeting. By
nine at night that muscle is spent — and that's exactly when you find yourself thirty minutes deep in
a stranger's vacation photos. You didn't get weaker through the day. You just ran out."
**[PATTERN INTERRUPT:** a battery icon draining to red]**
**VO:** "Relying on willpower to beat an app is like trying to outrun a treadmill that speeds up every
time you slow down. The machine never gets tired. You always do."
> You'll never out-discipline a machine that never gets tired.

### [1:55–3:00] BODY 2 — Change the environment: the Friction Stack
**VO:** "So if effort is the wrong tool, what's the right one? Distance. Behavior follows the path of
least resistance, and right now your phone is the easiest thing in the room — unlocked in one touch,
the app one tap away. So stack small barriers in the way. Log out, so the app asks for a password.
Bury it off the home screen, so you have to search for it. Turn the screen to grayscale, so the feed
stops glowing at you. None of these stop you. Each one just makes scrolling a little more annoying
than not scrolling. That's the Friction Stack — and annoyance, not discipline, is what actually wins."
**[ON-SCREEN:** The Friction Stack — log out · bury the app · grayscale · phone in another room]**
> Make the good choice easy and the bad one annoying. The rest takes care of itself.

### [3:00–3:35] MID-ROLL RE-HOOK
**VO:** "But here's what most 'digital detox' advice gets wrong. It tells you to add ten rules and
white-knuckle all of them. That's just willpower wearing a costume. The whole point is to do less, not
more — and there's one move that does more than every app-blocker combined."

### [3:35–4:30] BODY 3 — The one move  (open-loop payoff)
**VO:** "Put your phone in another room. That's it. Not face-down on the desk — another room, with a
door between you. Because the strongest trigger to check isn't a notification. It's just seeing the
phone sitting there. Out of sight, and the urge quietly fades, because your brain stops being reminded
to want it. When you need real focus, the phone doesn't get a seat at the table. It waits in the hall."
**[ON-SCREEN:** The one move — phone in another room, not face-down]**
**VO:** "You're not resisting the phone. You're just not in the room with it. That's not weakness
beaten by force. It's a fight you refused to start."
> Distance beats discipline. Every time.

### [4:30–5:00] CLOSE — PAYOFF + CTA + NEXT EPISODE
**VO:** "So — you can't stop doomscrolling by trying harder, because willpower runs out and the feed
never does. The fix isn't more self-control. It's more friction. That's the new rule: change the
environment, and the habit changes with it. Your move today: pick one barrier from the Friction Stack
and set it up before this video ends. Next, we go from removing distraction to summoning focus — how
to drop into deep flow on command, without waiting to feel inspired. Episode 11 shows you how.
Subscribe so you don't miss it. New rules only."
