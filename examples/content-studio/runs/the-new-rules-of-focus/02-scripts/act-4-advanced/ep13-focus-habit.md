**OWNS THE QUESTION:** How do I build lasting focus?
**KEYWORD:** build a focus habit · **OLD RULE:** "Overhaul everything" → **NEW RULE:** "Shrink it,

### [0:00–0:12] COLD-OPEN HOOK
**VO:** "Every January you rebuild your whole life. New app, new planner, new 5am routine. And every
February, it's gone. The problem was never your willpower. You tried to install a habit the exact way
habits don't form. Today, the way they do."
**[ON-SCREEN:** BUILD FOCUS THAT LASTS]** · [a towering resolution checklist crumbling to a single line]

### [0:12–0:35] GEO ANSWER BLOCK  ← citation target
**VO:** "Here's the short version. Lasting focus isn't built by overhauling your life — it's built by
one tiny habit that survives your worst days. Big overhauls fail because they run on motivation, and
motivation always fades. The habits that stick are small enough to do when you feel terrible,
attached to something you already do, and rewarded the instant you finish. Shrink it, anchor it,
celebrate it. That's the new rule for building focus that lasts."
**[ON-SCREEN:** Shrink it → Anchor it → Celebrate it]**

### [0:35–0:52] VALUE PROMISE + OPEN LOOP
**VO:** "In the next few minutes: why big overhauls are almost designed to fail, the three moves that
make a focus habit stick, and the one most people skip — the one your brain actually needs to lock it
in. Stay to the end, because the last step is the one that decides whether this habit is still around
in a month."

### [0:52–1:55] BODY 1 — Why overhauls fail
**VO:** "First, why the fresh-start approach keeps betraying you. A big overhaul depends on motivation
and energy — and both are highest the day you start, and lower every day after. So you've designed a
habit that needs your best self to show up every single day. But habits are for your worst days. The
day you're tired, stressed, and behind — that's the real test. And a ninety-minute deep-work block
never survives it."
**[PATTERN INTERRUPT:** a giant morning routine collapsing into one small line of text]**
**VO:** "The 5am, cold-plunge, two-hour-routine lasts exactly as long as the excitement does. Then
you're back where you started — and now you feel like a failure on top of it."
> A habit that needs your best day will die on your first bad one.

### [1:55–3:00] BODY 2 — Shrink it, then anchor it
**VO:** "So build for the bad day instead. Two moves. First, shrink it — until it's almost
embarrassingly small. Not 'an hour of focused work.' One page. Not 'meditate twenty minutes.' Three
breaths. So small you can't talk yourself out of it. So small it survives the day you feel awful. The
size isn't the point yet — showing up is. Second, anchor it to something you already do without
thinking. After I pour my morning coffee, I read one page. The habit you already have becomes the
reminder — so you're not relying on memory or motivation to trigger it."
**[ON-SCREEN:** Shrink it below the resistance · Anchor it to an existing habit]**
> Don't find time for the habit. Bolt it onto a habit you already have.

### [3:00–3:35] MID-ROLL RE-HOOK
**VO:** "But here's the step almost everyone skips — and it's the one that actually wires the habit
in. Miss it, and even a tiny, anchored habit quietly fades. Get it right, and within a couple of
weeks the whole thing starts to feel automatic."

### [3:35–4:30] BODY 3 — Celebrate it + The Stick Stack  (open-loop payoff)
**VO:** "The third move is celebrate it — immediately. The instant you finish your one page, feel
good about it. A quiet 'yes,' a nod, a genuine hit of satisfaction. It feels silly. It's also how the
brain decides a behavior is worth repeating: a reward on the spot tells it, do that again. Skip the
reward, and the habit has no reason to stick. Put the three together and you've got what I call the
Stick Stack — shrink it, anchor it, celebrate it."
**[ON-SCREEN:** The Stick Stack — Shrink → Anchor → Celebrate]**
**VO:** "Small enough to survive a bad day. Attached to a trigger you can't forget. Rewarded so your
brain locks it in. That's a habit that holds."
> You don't rise to your goals. You fall to the size of the habit you can repeat on a bad day.

### [4:30–5:00] CLOSE — PAYOFF + CTA + NEXT EPISODE
**VO:** "So — lasting focus isn't built by overhauling everything. It's built by one habit small
enough to survive your worst day, anchored to something you already do, and celebrated the second you
finish. That's the new rule: shrink it, anchor it, celebrate it — the Stick Stack. Your move today:
pick one focus habit, shrink it to something almost too easy, and bolt it to a thing you already do.
Next, we take on the last myth in this series — that discipline means being hard on yourself. It's

so you don't miss it. New rules only."
