## Core thesis (your franchise device)
The **old rules of focus** ("try harder", "more willpower", "just be disciplined") were written for a
world that wasn't engineered to distract you. In 2026 they fail — so each episode retires an **Old
Rule** and installs a **New Rule** built for a dopamine economy.

## Quotable, sourced ideas (GEO citation fuel — attribute; re-verify at publish)
- **Procrastination is emotion management, not time management** — the brain's alarm system avoiding discomfort.
- **Dopamine + temporal discounting:** the reward system prioritizes immediate over distant rewards;
  low dopamine → the brain can't filter distraction.
- **Variable reward schedules:** social platforms use the same mechanism as slot machines — you keep
  scrolling for the unpredictable hit.
- **Amygdala vs prefrontal cortex:** the threat-detector flags a hard task as danger → avoidance; the
  planning brain is often outpaced.
- **Digital overstimulation** trains the brain "for speed, not depth"; moving to a low-dopamine task
  feels like withdrawal.
- **Systems beat motivation:** discipline is reducing the need to decide; **start small** (shrink,
  anchor, celebrate).
- **Deep Work (Newport):** focus is now rare and valuable — a competitive superpower.
- **2026 finding:** high trait procrastination predicts increased *anticipatory anxiety* about goals.
