# Shorts Plan — The New Rules of Focus

Same system as Series 1: cut 2–3 Shorts per episode (the hook, the surprising claim, the one flip).
20–40s, hook in 1–2s, purple/cream + "101" badge, caption leads with the question, end card → the
long video/playlist. Post between long uploads to stay in-feed daily.

| Ep | Short A (the surprising claim) | Short B (the flip) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Your attention span isn't broken — it's being farmed." | Don't touch the feed for the first 30 minutes. |
| 2 | "Procrastination isn't laziness. It's fear." | Shrink the task below the fear, then start. |
| 3 | "Your feed uses the same trick as a slot machine." | Name the design, and it loses its grip. |
| 4 | "Every quick hit resets your baseline higher." | Lower the dopamine floor; boring stops feeling boring. |
| 5 | "You don't lack willpower — the task feels like a threat." | Make step one laughably small. |
| 6 | "Motivation is for starting. Systems are for finishing." | Remove the decision, not the desire. |
| 7 | "Multitasking isn't doing more — it's switching, badly." | Single-task; the switch is the tax. |
| 8 | "Discipline doesn't beat procrastination. Tiny does." | The 2-minute start rule. |
| 9 | "Focus is now rarer — which makes it a superpower." | Protect one deep-work block a day. |
| 10 | "You can't out-willpower a phone built to win." | Change the environment, not your self-control. |
| 11 | "Flow isn't luck. It's conditions you can set." | Engineer the trigger; stop waiting for inspiration. |
| 12 | "Boredom is the gym for your attention." | Let yourself be bored on purpose. |
| 13 | "Willpower fades. Habits don't." | Shrink it, anchor it, celebrate it. |
| 14 | "Beating yourself up makes focus worse, not better." | Self-compassion out-performs self-criticism. |
| 15 | "The old rules of focus were written before the feed." | One new rule to start today. |

Batch-cut in the same session you finalize each script. Ep 1 & Ep 15 get all 3 Short types.
