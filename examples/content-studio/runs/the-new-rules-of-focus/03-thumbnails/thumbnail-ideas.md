## System notes
- Yellow accent on the key word (`CAN'T`, `NOT`, `LIE`, `TINY`, `DEEP`).
- Several are "Old vs New" — use a crossed-out old thing + a glowing new thing.
- Ep 1 & Ep 15 get 3 variants + A/B. 1280×720; legible at ~210px. Reuse one master template
  (purple bg + cream "101" badge + text style), swap the focal visual per episode.
- Cross-brand with Series 1 (Money): identical badge + palette so a viewer who found one season
  recognizes the other on Browse.
