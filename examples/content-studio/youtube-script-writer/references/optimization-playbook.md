# Growth Optimization Playbook — subscribers, viewers, followers

"The best optimization" is not one trick — it's a stack. Ordered by leverage (do the top ones
first). Each is concrete and measurable.

## 1. Packaging is 50% of growth (highest leverage)
- Title + thumbnail decide CTR, which decides whether the algorithm even tries the video.
- Draft **3 title/thumbnail options per video**; use YouTube's Test & Compare for thumbnails.
- Target **CTR ≥ 4–6%** on browse; if a video has good retention but low CTR, it's a
  packaging problem — re-thumbnail it.

## 2. Retention is what compounds
- First 30 seconds decide the video. Track the **intro retention** and the biggest drop-off;
  rewrite those spots next time.
- Aim for **Average % viewed ≥ 45–50%** (short) / **≥ 35%** (long). Above-average retention →
  the algorithm expands the impressions.

## 3. Series binge = session time = channel-wide lift
- Publish as a **playlist series**; end-screen each episode into the next.
- Sequence so Eps 1–3 (highest demand) pull viewers into the deeper catalog.
- Session time lifts *every* video, not just the one watched — this is the series' superpower.

## 4. The Shorts → long-form funnel
- Cut 2–3 **Shorts** from each long video's best 20–40s moments (the hook, a surprising stat,
  a demo). Shorts get reach; the pinned comment + end card route to the long video/series.
- Shorts grow *followers* fast; long-form grows *watch time*. Use both deliberately.

## 5. Subscriber conversion moments
- Subscribes come from a **value spike + a specific reason**, not "smash subscribe."
- Best moments: right after you deliver a promised payoff, and at the end pointing to the next
  episode. Target **subs-per-video and "subscribers from this video"** in analytics.

## 6. GEO distribution (compounding, most creators skip it)
- Repurpose each transcript into a **companion article/FAQ** (blog/Medium/LinkedIn), first 200
  words = the answer, FAQ from real research questions, video embedded, playlist linked.
- Goal: get **cited by AI answer engines** → readers click through → subscribers you'd never
  reach on YouTube alone. This is the "visitors/followers" flywheel beyond YouTube itself.

## 7. Community & engagement loops
- Ask **one specific question** in the video → pin it as a comment → reply to early comments in
  the first hour (engagement velocity signals the algorithm).
- Use the Community tab for polls between episodes to keep the audience warm.

## 8. Cadence & consistency
- Pick a sustainable schedule (e.g. 1–2/week) and hold it; the algorithm rewards predictability.
- Batch-produce the series so cadence never slips.

## The 5 analytics to watch (with target benchmarks)
| Metric | Why | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Click-through rate (CTR) | Packaging health | 4–6%+ |
| Average % viewed / view duration | Retention (master signal) | ≥45% short / ≥35% long |
| Impressions & "traffic source" mix | Is the algorithm promoting it (Browse/Suggested)? | Browse+Suggested rising |
| Subscribers per video | Conversion | Trend up per series |
| Returning vs. new viewers | Series/binge health | Returning share rising |

## 30-day growth sprint (how to deploy the series)
1. Publish Ep 1 (flagship); cut 3 Shorts from it; draft its companion article.
2. Reply to every comment in hour 1; pin the question.
3. Publish on cadence; end-screen each into the next.
4. Day 7: check CTR/retention; re-thumbnail any low-CTR/high-retention video.
5. Day 14/30: double down on the format/topic with the best retention; plan series #2 from the
   winning thread.
