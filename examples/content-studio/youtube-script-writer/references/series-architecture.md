# 15-Episode Series Architecture

A series is not 15 standalone videos — it's a **bingeable arc** engineered for session time
and subscriber conversion. Early episodes hook and convert; middle episodes build authority
and topical depth (great for GEO); late episodes deliver the big payoff that makes the series
worth finishing and sharing.

## The arc (15 episodes, 5 acts)

| Act | Eps | Role | Design intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Hook Cluster** | 1–3 | Broad, high-search, high-curiosity entry points | Win the algorithm's first impression; convert new viewers to subs. Ep 1 is the flagship. |
| **2 · Foundations** | 4–7 | Core concepts, definitions, "how it works" | Own the GEO question surface; build topical authority; searchable evergreen. |
| **3 · Application** | 8–11 | Step-by-step, tools, workflows, demos | Highest practical value → shares, saves, comments; binge momentum. |
| **4 · Advanced / Scale** | 12–14 | Pro tactics, edge cases, mistakes, systems | Retain the committed viewer; signal expertise. |
| **5 · Payoff / Flagship** | 15 | Capstone: the big result / full build / case study | The reason to finish the series; most shareable; ties the loop shut. |

## Sequencing rules
- **Front-load demand:** Episodes 1–3 target the highest-search, lowest-friction queries so
  the series gets discovered; deeper/nichier topics go later.
- **Every episode owns exactly one GEO question** (from research's question surface). No two
  episodes compete for the same query.
- **Binge loops:** each episode's end screen points to the *next* one; the playlist order is
  the intended watch order.
- **Curiosity ladder:** each act raises the stakes/specificity so finishing feels rewarding.
- **Subscribe pacing:** hardest subscribe push in Eps 1–3 (new viewers); later episodes assume
  a subscriber and push community/next-series.

## Per-episode blueprint fields (fill all 15)
1. Episode # and act
2. SEO title (keyword front-loaded, ≤60 chars)
3. Target keyword + search intent
4. **GEO question owned** (the exact question people ask AI)
5. 10-second hook line
6. 5–7 beat outline
7. Subscribe moment
8. Thumbnail concept (≤3 words + visual)
9. Metadata: description first 2 lines, 8–12 tags, chapters
10. "Watch next" → which episode (binge loop)

## Coherence checks
- Titles form a recognizable "shelf" (consistent pattern + numbering, e.g. "Ep 4:").
- No topic gaps and no overlaps across the 15 questions owned.
- Difficulty/specificity increases monotonically across acts.
- The flagship (Ep 1) and capstone (Ep 15) are the two strongest thumbnails.
