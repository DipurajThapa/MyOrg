## What's already working (keep doing)
1. **Distinct brand identity.** The purple/cream "101" system is memorable and consistent — most
   new channels have none. This is an asset to build a "shelf" on.
2. **A repeatable hook formula.** "Stop [common belief] → Do THIS instead" is a proven,
   curiosity-driven, contrarian pattern that travels well and suits Shorts.
3. **Psychology angle = shareable + GEO-rich.** "Why one word ruins your mood" is exactly the
   kind of question people ask AI engines — huge untapped GEO opportunity (see below).
4. **Tight run-time.** ~5 min is retention-friendly for essay content and easy to cut into Shorts.
5. **Monetization intent early.** The Courses tab shows you're thinking about the funnel from day one.

## Gaps & opportunities (ranked by leverage at your stage)
1. **No catalog depth or series structure (biggest issue).** 3 standalone videos give the
   algorithm almost nothing to recommend and no *session* to build. At 10 subs the game is
   **volume + consistency + a bingeable series**, not per-video optimization. → Publish a themed
   **season** where each episode feeds the next.
2. **Titles are curiosity-only; add a search/GEO anchor.** "Stop Trying to Look Like a Leader"
   is great curiosity but owns no query. Pair the curiosity with the real question people ask
   ("…(the psychology of real authority)") so the video wins Search *and* Browse.
3. **No Shorts funnel.** Your 5-min essays are perfect raw material. From zero subs, Shorts are
   the fastest follower engine — cut 2–3 per video (the hook, the counterintuitive turn, the payoff).
4. **No GEO distribution.** Self-improvement questions are among the most-asked to ChatGPT/
   Perplexity ("how do I stop caring what people think," "why do I procrastinate"). A companion
   article per video (first 200 words = the answer) can get the channel cited by AI engines and
   pull in readers YouTube alone won't reach.
5. **Playlists unused.** Group videos into a named series playlist so binge-watching lifts the
   whole channel (session time is channel-wide).
6. **Packaging consistency.** Lock a thumbnail template (your purple/cream + 2–3 word hook) so
   the catalog looks like one franchise on the browse shelf.

## The single biggest lever right now
**Pick ONE tight sub-lane and publish a consistent, bingeable 15-episode series in it — with
Shorts cut from each and a companion article per episode.** That converts a scattered 3-video
start into a catalog the algorithm can promote, a session viewers can binge, and a body of
answers AI engines can cite. This is exactly what the `youtube-script-writer` produces — and
"The New Rules of Money" (extending your best-fit theme, video #3) is the sharpest place to start.

## Can it be better than it's doing today?
**Yes — decisively, because it's at the starting line with good bones.** The brand and the hook
formula are already better than most channels ever get; what's missing is *catalog, consistency,
series structure, Shorts, and GEO* — all mechanical, all fixable. The ceiling here is high.

- 3 videos is a small sample for niche inference — treated as directional, not definitive.
